**Recommendation: make *weekly ordering customers* our north-star metric.** Definition: distinct real (non-internal) users with at least one delivered order in a Monday–Sunday week, in IST.

**Why not the alternatives?** Downloads grew 52% from June to August, but a download isn't value: only a minority of new users are still active a week later. GMV rose from ₹0.6 lakh to ₹1.9 lakh a week, but it mixes how many people order with basket size and price, so discounts or a few large orders can move it. DAU counts browsing; notifications can raise it without anyone eating.

Weekly ordering customers grew from 140 to 375, faster than weekly actives, which shows real value delivered. But D7 retention is flat, so habit-building is the gap this metric will keep in view.

**Every team can move it** through the metric tree: Growth (new users), CRM (resurrected users), Discovery (view-to-cart), Checkout and Payments (conversion, starting with web at 58% vs android at 66%).

**Guardrails:** median delivery time, cancellation rate and contribution margin per order, so we can't buy the metric with discounts or rushed deliveries.

**Next step:** set a Q4 target of 600 weekly ordering customers by December, and review the tree weekly.

The Diwali sale delivered ₹25.4 lakh in 13 days, about four times September's daily revenue (₹1.96 lakh vs ₹0.48 lakh a day). Growth came from both more orders and bigger baskets: average order value rose from ₹2,829 to ₹3,747, led by electronics at two-thirds of revenue. Two things need attention. Discounts averaged 25%, three times normal, so we should confirm the sale was margin-positive. COD orders were cancelled at 12.4%, more than double the prepaid rate. Next year, I recommend offering a small UPI cashback to COD buyers during the sale to cut cancellations.
